ROSHPAD DOCS

A launchpad on Robinhood Chain where every token trades against a Stock Token and holders earn that stock from trading fees

01 · overviewMarkets quoted in stocks

Every token launched on Roshpad is paired with one Robinhood Stock Token instead of ETH or dollars: NVDA, TSLA, SPY, AAPL and 14 more. The pair is chosen at launch and never changes. Prices, fees and holder rewards are all in that stock.

Launching, trading, collecting fees and claiming rewards are transactions from your wallet to the contracts. No server holds funds and no keeper is needed.

02 · createOne transaction

Pick a name, a ticker, a logo, the Stock Token to trade against and a trading fee. The page saves the metadata, then the launch transaction creates the token, opens the pool and adds all of the liquidity. An optional first buy in ETH lands in the same transaction.

03 · liquidity$5,000 to $35,000

The launch reads the Chainlink price of the chosen stock and opens the pool at a $5,000 market cap measured in that stock. The whole supply goes into two single-sided positions on the pool:

├── curve              800M tokens from a $5,000 to a $35,000 market cap, a 7x price range
├── above the curve    200M tokens from $35,000 to the top of the price range
├── start              the pool starts at the bottom edge, so both positions hold only the token
├── price              fixed in the pair stock at launch: if the stock moves, the market cap in dollars moves with it

Nothing happens at $35,000: no migration and no graduation. Trading simply continues into the upper range.

04 · permanentLiquidity can never be removed

Both positions are owned by the launchpad contract directly on the Uniswap v4 PoolManager. The contract has no function that removes or reduces liquidity, and it cannot be upgraded. The only thing it can do with a position is collect its trading fees.

05 · routerBuy with ETH, USDG or the stock

├── with the stock    straight into the market pool
├── with USDG         USDG to the stock on Uniswap v3, then into the market
├── with ETH          ETH to USDG and USDG to the stock on Uniswap v3, then into the market
├── selling           the same routes back, with no approval transaction: the token trusts the Roshpad router
├── slippage          you pick 1%, 3% or 5%; the transaction reverts if the final amount is lower

06 · splitWhere fees go

Uniswap v4 charges the fee on the token going in. Buys pay it in the pair stock, sells pay it in the market token. Anyone can collect a market's fees at any time.

07 · earnHold the token, earn the stock

The holder share of every collection is sent to the token contract, which records rewards per token for everyone holding at that moment. Your share is exact and waits for you: claim it whenever you like, or anyone can pay a list of holders in one transaction. Selling or sending tokens later does not take away rewards you already earned.

├── who earns          every wallet holding the token, however small
├── who never earns    the pool, the launchpad, the router and the burn address
├── claim              from the market page or the Rewards tab
├── pay all holders    one button that pushes rewards to the listed holders; anyone can press it
├── waiting in pool    fees not collected yet; the page shows your estimated share

08 · onchainThree contracts

RoshLaunchpad creates every token as an EIP-1167 clone of RoshToken, opens its Uniswap v4 pool and holds the liquidity. RoshRouter trades for users. RoshToken is the ERC-20 with the reward accounting.

10 · questionsQuestions

Do I need the Stock Token to buy?No. Pay with ETH or USDG and the router converts on the way in.
When do I get paid?Whenever fees are collected. Anyone can collect, and your share then waits in the token contract until you claim or someone pays all holders.
Can the creator rug the pool?No. The liquidity belongs to the launchpad contract, which has no way to remove it.
What are Stock Tokens?Tokenised debt securities issued by Robinhood that track a share price. They are not the shares themselves and are restricted in some jurisdictions, including the US.
Are the contracts audited?Not yet. Use amounts you are fine losing.
